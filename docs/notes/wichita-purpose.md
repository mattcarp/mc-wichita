# Wichita: purpose (corrected 2026-10-13)

Wichita (mattcarp/mc-wichita) exists to help the vulnerable. It runs sentiment and stress analysis on voices heard over local radio frequencies, received with software-defined radio. It is receive-only: it never transmits.

Wichita is NOT about loneliness. Loneliness is the central theme of the Hank project (mattcarp/mc-homecompanion). Earlier drafts that framed Wichita around loneliness ("Wichita listens for the ones who never say mayday", "loneliness as a channel problem") were wrong and have been removed from this repo.

The name comes from Glen Campbell's "Wichita Lineman": the man on the wire who hears more than the line carries. For Wichita that means the distress or vulnerability in a voice, below the words. See MISSION.md.
