# Wichita: loneliness definitions (draft)

Status: draft, not committed to mattcarp/mc-wichita. Captured from Mattie on 2026-10-09.

## 1. Loneliness as signal-to-noise

Picture a receiver tuned to a frequency nobody broadcasts on. The silence is not empty: it is full of signals that cannot be decoded. Noise is information without a matched code. Loneliness is signals with no shared protocol.

## 2. Loneliness as a channel problem

Loneliness = lots of information + a bad channel + no receptors.

Example: love, and people from the past whose channel went quiet. The information is still there; the link that carried it is gone.

## 3. Context

- Wichita Lineman: "I can hear you through the whine" and "I'll be here for all time". The lineman listens for one voice inside the noise on the line, and stays.
- Eleanor Rigby as steganography: the rice is leftover signal, and Father McKenzie is the archivist.
- Sotto Sound siblings: Hank holds the gone; Wichita hears the never-mayday.

## Scope

Wichita helps people. It does not cure loneliness.

# Themes from the 2026-10-09 call (draft)

Status: draft record, not committed and not sent anywhere. Captured from Mattie's call on the night of 2026-10-08/09 to guide future thinking on Wichita and Hank.

## A. The information-theory framing

Loneliness is not a shortage of information. It is too much of it (love, memories, things to say) with a bad channel and no distribution points or endpoints to route it to. The information has nowhere to land.

The buildable piece is the distribution point. Adding a place for the signal to be received and routed turns a dead channel into a path. That is something software and AI can actually build.

## B. Wichita Lineman as the emotional core

"I can hear you through the whine" and "I'll be here for all time." A voice carrying through noise, and a listener who stays. That is the promise of Wichita: a receive-only listener that keeps hearing, and keeps being there.

## C. Eleanor Rigby as steganography

The rice in the church is leftover signal that nobody sees. Father McKenzie is the archivist of the invisible, tending what no one else notices.

## D. Piano Man: loneliness shared

"Sharing a drink they call loneliness, but it's better than drinking alone." Loneliness can be something shared rather than solitary. Being lonely next to someone is already a kind of channel.

## E. The mission arc

Wichita listens for the ones who never say mayday. Hank holds the ones already gone. Between them is the whole arc, from the signal you can still catch to the one you can't.

## F. Mattie's framing

The aim is not to cure loneliness but to help people. Quantify it in information-theory terms (information, channel, endpoints) so it can be addressed with AI and programming.
